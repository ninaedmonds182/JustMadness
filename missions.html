🐾 The Great Dog Baking School

Est. 1923 · Ages 8–12 · Mild · Mad · Bonkers

Dog Baking School is in trouble.

The secret recipe has disappeared.

Professor Baker insists everything is under control.

Biscuit strongly disagrees.

Now a new team of bakers has been recruited. Their job is simple:

Find the missing recipe.

Unfortunately, Professor Baker has made this considerably more complicated than it needs to be.

The children will follow Biscuit through a series of ridiculous training missions, uncover clues, crack codes, survive Professor Baker's inventions and recover the missing recipe.

And when they finally have everything they need...

they bake the cake.

How long does it last? That's up to you.

Short on time? Choose decorate only — the mission runs around 2.5 hours.

Want the full experience? Choose bake the cake — and settle in for 3–4 hours of children fully occupied.

You're not playing six separate games.

You're joining Dog Baking School.

Each mission is another part of the story. The children don't know what the next challenge will be until they unlock it.

A short video briefing introduces what has happened and what they need to do.

Then the device goes down.

The children take over.

They search, solve, carry, dress, dig, freeze, race and generally cause the sort of trouble Professor Baker probably should have expected.

Every successful mission reveals another piece of the recipe.

Until, eventually, there's only one thing left to do.

Bake it.

Six missions. One very strange school.

Each mission unlocks the next. No peeking ahead.

Mission 01
The Great Fetch Mission
The first clue is somewhere in the school.
Biscuit needs a team who can work together, move quickly and retrieve it before it's lost again. It's probably best not to ask where it came from.
Mission 02
The Water-Powered Recipe Vault
The next clue is locked inside Professor Baker's completely unnecessary water-powered vault.
Work together. Move the water. Crack the code. Try not to flood the bakery.
Mission 03
The Doggy Dressing Room
Every proper dog baker needs a proper dog identity.
Unfortunately, the school's supply of sensible clothing has completely disappeared.
Find the hidden codes and discover who you're becoming.
Mission 04
The Everything Bowl
Professor Baker claims his ingredient storage system is "perfectly organised." Biscuit calls it a pile.
Somewhere inside it is another missing clue.
Mission 05
Operation Chill
The final recipe clue has gone somewhere very cold.
The children will need teamwork, patience and a little scientific thinking to recover it.
Professor Baker calls this "cryogenic recipe protection." Biscuit calls it "putting a recipe in ice."
Mission 06
The Dog Run
One final mission stands between the bakers and the complete recipe.
Move fast. Work together. Recover the last clue.
Then there's nothing left to do...
Except bake.

While the children are on the mission...

You're running the operation from behind the scenes.

The children see the adventure. You see the setup instructions.

While they're completing one mission, you prepare the next. The app tells you what needs to be ready and when.

Before the party, everything you need is waiting for you: the shopping list, equipment list and step-by-step setup instructions.

You don't have to invent the party.

You're bringing an adventure that's already been created to life.

Choose how far you take the madness.

Mild

The story and missions stay the same, with a simpler setup and everyday equipment.

Mad

More props. More ridiculousness. A little more preparation. The sweet spot for most parties.

Bonkers

The full Dog Baking School experience. Bigger setups. More chaos. More ridiculous moments.

The children get the same adventure. You decide how far you want to take it.

Choose your finale.

🥣 Bake the cake

The children mix, bake and decorate the cake together. The full experience — around 3–4 hours depending on difficulty level.

🎂 Decorate the cake

Have the cakes ready beforehand and let the children turn them into their final creation. A quicker party — around 2.5–3 hours.

Either way, the story ends the same way: the recipe is recovered. The cake is complete. Dog Baking School survives another year.

No baking experience needed. The app guides you through whichever option you choose.

Good to know

✔ One supermarket trip covers everything. Most equipment is already at home.
✔ Works indoors, outdoors, garden, hall or kitchen.
✔ Teams are assigned with wristbands. You can let the app assign teams or choose them yourself.
✔ Every child receives a certificate at the end.
